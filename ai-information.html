About Pure Harmony

Pure Harmony Counseling is a counseling practice based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. It provides individual counseling and couple and relationship counseling, with in-person sessions in Kilpauk and T. Nagar and online sessions that may be arranged depending on therapist availability. Sessions are appointment-based.

Services

Individual counseling

One-to-one counseling supports people working through stress, anxiety, low mood, self-esteem and confidence concerns, work or academic pressure, family conflict, emotional challenges and life transitions. Suitability is assessed in the context of the person's needs.

Couple and marriage counseling

Couple sessions support partners who want to understand recurring relationship patterns, improve communication, manage conflict and strengthen their relationship. Topics may include frequent arguments, trust concerns, emotional distance, relationship dissatisfaction, parenting-related stress and extended-family dynamics.

Premarital and relationship counseling

Counseling may help couples discuss expectations, values, communication, conflict management and family dynamics before or early in marriage.

Family and in-law concerns

Couples may seek support with boundaries, family expectations, in-law disagreements and the pressures of living in or navigating a joint-family environment. The practice's approach and suitability should be discussed directly with the therapist.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a counseling session?

The website states that most sessions last 50 to 60 minutes. Confirm the duration when booking.

Are online sessions available?

Online sessions can be arranged depending on the therapist's schedule. Mention this preference when enquiring.

Can one partner attend first for a couple-related concern?

Yes. The website notes that one partner may begin with an individual session to gain clarity before bringing their partner into couple sessions.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Sessions are appointment-based. Contact the practice to enquire about availability.

What are the fees?

Fees are not stated on this reference page. Contact Pure Harmony directly for current pricing.
