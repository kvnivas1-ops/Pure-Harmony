A marriage simulator for real-life relationship decisions

Marriage is shaped by everyday decisions as much as by the big milestones. How do you respond when family expectations clash with couple time? How do you make financial decisions when both partners have responsibilities to their parents? What happens when you disagree about parenting, household work, career opportunities or boundaries with relatives?

The Pure Harmony Marriage Simulator is a free, interactive relationship experience for couples. It presents ten hypothetical situations inspired by common questions and pressures that can arise in married life. You choose how you would respond, your partner answers independently, and you can then use the differences as a starting point for a more thoughtful conversation.

What does the marriage compatibility simulator explore?

The scenarios are rooted in the realities many Indian couples navigate, including relationships with in-laws, family traditions, financial responsibilities, parenting expectations and the balance between individual ambitions and shared plans. They also explore trust, privacy, conflict repair, emotional support and the invisible work involved in running a home.

  • Family boundaries and relationships with in-laws
  • Money, savings and responsibilities towards parents
  • Parenting choices and extended-family expectations
  • Career decisions and making room for both partners
  • Household responsibilities and emotional labour
  • Communication, disagreements and repairing conflict
  • Protecting couple time while staying connected to family

How to use the simulator with your partner

Answer each situation honestly rather than choosing what sounds like the ideal response. Invite your partner to complete the same situations independently. When you compare answers, look beyond whether you selected the same option. Ask what each person was thinking about, what they wanted to protect and what would help them feel understood. A different answer can open up a useful conversation about expectations that may not have come up otherwise.

Created by Ashwini Rao, Counselling Psychologist

Ashwini Rao is the founder of Pure Harmony and a counselling psychologist with an M.Phil. in Psychology and over eight years of experience. She developed this marriage simulator using her experience working with couples, alongside research and reflection on the relationship challenges that arise in everyday life. The aim is to help partners notice assumptions, understand each other's perspectives and make space for conversations before small differences become recurring sources of friction.

What your compatibility score means (and what it doesn't)

Your result shows how closely your selected responses matched across these ten hypothetical situations. It is a conversation prompt, not a clinical assessment, psychological diagnosis or scientifically validated measure of compatibility. It cannot predict whether a marriage will succeed. Couples can have different instincts and still build a healthy relationship through respect, communication and a willingness to work through decisions together.

Frequently asked questions

Is the marriage simulator free?

Yes. The Pure Harmony Marriage Simulator is free to play and does not require an account to begin.

Is this a marriage compatibility test?

It is an interactive relationship exercise that compares how two people respond to selected situations. It is not a formal or validated compatibility test and should not be used to judge a relationship.

Are the questions relevant to Indian couples?

The situations are designed around experiences that may be familiar to Indian couples, such as navigating in-law relationships, family obligations, festivals, money decisions, parenting expectations and balancing couple time with extended-family needs.

Can the simulator replace couple counselling?

No. The simulator can help you identify topics worth discussing, but it cannot provide personalised guidance or replace counselling. If you would like support working through a relationship concern, you can book a session with Pure Harmony.
