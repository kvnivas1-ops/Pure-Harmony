Premarital Counselling in Chennai

Getting married is exciting, but it also brings decisions and expectations that can be difficult to discuss openly. Premarital counselling gives you and your partner time to understand each other more deeply before you begin married life.

You may agree on the big things but have not discussed everyday realities: money, household responsibilities, career plans, children, where you will live, relationships with parents or how you handle disagreements. Avoiding these conversations now can make them harder later.

Does any of this feel familiar?

If these experiences sound close to what you are going through, you do not have to figure everything out on your own.

  • You want to discuss expectations before the wedding, not after conflict begins.
  • You have different views about finances, work, children or household roles.
  • You are unsure how to balance your families and your relationship.
  • You find it difficult to disagree without worrying about the future.
  • You want to build communication habits before married life begins.

How counselling may help

Premarital counselling is not a test that decides whether you should marry. It is a guided conversation about compatibility, expectations, strengths and areas that need discussion. Ashwini can help you explore how you communicate, approach conflict, make decisions and understand each other's needs.

Understanding your relationship and family context

For many couples in Chennai, marriage also connects two families. Expectations around ceremonies, living arrangements, caring for parents, traditions, finances and family involvement can be significant. These topics can be discussed respectfully while keeping the couple's own priorities visible.

Is this the right time to seek support?

Premarital counselling can be useful for engaged couples, couples planning a wedding or partners considering a long-term commitment. You do not need to be facing a major problem to benefit from thoughtful preparation.

Frequently asked questions

When should we start premarital counselling?

You can begin whenever you are ready to have meaningful conversations. Starting before wedding planning becomes overwhelming may give you more time to reflect.

What topics are covered?

Common topics include communication, conflict, finances, family expectations, roles, parenting, values, intimacy and future plans. The focus is tailored to your concerns.

Do both partners need to attend?

Yes, premarital counselling is generally designed for both partners to participate together.
