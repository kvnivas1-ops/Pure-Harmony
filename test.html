What actually changes

Therapy is not about deciding who was right. It is about changing the pattern that keeps putting you both on opposite sides.

"You never listen to me." → "I feel invisible when this happens."
Walking away mid-argument → Pausing, then returning to finish it
Keeping score of past hurts → Repairing so they stop resurfacing
Avoiding the hard conversation → Having it, safely, with a guide present

How it works

Four steps. No paperwork maze, no call centre.

  1. 1

    A short WhatsApp conversation

    You message Ashwini directly. She replies personally, understands your situation briefly, and tells you honestly whether couple therapy is the right fit.

  2. 2

    Your first session together

    Both partners attend. Each of you gets uninterrupted space to say what has been hard. Ashwini listens for the pattern underneath.

  3. 3

    Individual conversations

    Sometimes one session each, so things that are difficult to say in front of your partner can still be understood and worked with.

  4. 4

    Working the pattern

    Ongoing sessions with practical tools you use between visits. Progress is reviewed openly — you always know where you stand.

Many couples start with one partner alone. That is completely valid, and often where clarity begins.

Questions couples usually ask

Will the therapist take my partner's side?

No. Ashwini's role is to be useful to the relationship, not to award points. Both of you get equal space, and if one person is dominating the room, she will gently rebalance it.

My partner refuses to come. Is there any point?

Yes. A great deal of useful work happens with one partner. You will understand your own patterns better, respond differently, and that alone shifts the dynamic. Many reluctant partners join later once they see the change.

How many sessions will we need?

It depends on what you are working through. After the first two or three sessions Ashwini will give you an honest sense of the road ahead rather than an open-ended commitment.

Is everything we say confidential?

Yes. Sessions are held under strict professional confidentiality. What is discussed in individual conversations is handled with care and is never used against either partner.

We are not married. Can we still come?

Absolutely. Dating couples, engaged couples, long-distance partners and those in long-term partnerships are all welcome. Pre-marital sessions are among the most valuable work we do.

What if we decide to separate?

Therapy does not force an outcome. Some couples reconnect, some arrive at a clear and respectful ending. Either way, you get there with understanding instead of resentment.
