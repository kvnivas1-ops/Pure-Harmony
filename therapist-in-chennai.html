What people in Chennai bring into the room

Across 300+ clients, certain themes keep turning up in this city.

The marriage that works on paper

Two capable people, good jobs, a home that runs smoothly, and a distance neither of you can name out loud. This usually arrives disguised as a complaint about chores.

Constant low grade conflict

Same argument, different Tuesday. Nobody wins, nobody leaves, everybody is tired.

Family sitting in the middle of the marriage

In-laws, parents, timelines, expectations about children and where you live. In Chennai these are rarely side issues.

Trust that has broken

Not always an affair. Sometimes a financial secret, a repeatedly broken promise, or a discovery that reframes the last five years.

Anxiety that became background noise

The tightness you have stopped noticing because it has been there so long it feels like your personality.

Burnout that sleep does not fix

Common in Chennai's IT and healthcare corridors. Rest stops working when the problem is not actually tiredness.

Self-esteem and self-doubt

The voice that discounts every good thing and keeps a detailed record of every failure.

Postpartum emotional changes

Feeling unlike yourself after having a baby, and feeling guilty for feeling that way. This is far more common than most new mothers are told.

Grief you moved through instead of felt

Loss that got managed rather than mourned, because there were arrangements to make and people to hold together.

The conversations worth having before the wedding

Money, careers, children, where you will live, how much family involvement feels right to each of you. These questions are much cheaper to ask now than in year four.

Arranged or love, the same groundwork helps

Whether you met three months ago or grew up together, structured conversation surfaces assumptions neither of you knew you were making.

Understanding how each of you handles conflict

One person raises their voice, the other goes silent. Knowing this about each other in advance changes the first real fight completely.

About Ashwini Rao

She specialises in marriage counselling, relationship issues, communication breakdowns, trust concerns, postpartum emotional changes, and self-esteem difficulties. Couples work is the largest part of her practice, and individual therapy is a substantial part of it too.

Her style is warm, calm, and practical. Sessions are conversational rather than clinical, and the aim is never to hand you a five step plan in week one. It is to help you see clearly what is actually happening, and then work out what to do about it together.
